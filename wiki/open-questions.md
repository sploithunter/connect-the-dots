# Open research questions

[Wiki home](index.md) · [Source index](sources.md)

These are investigation directions, not additional factual graph edges. Retain the claim's provenance and identify the next record that would answer the question.

- Which legal investment vehicles held the early Anthropic and Conjecture stakes, and what rights accompanied them? Start with the [funding topic](topics/funding.md).
- Which SFF recommendations were paid, to which legal entities, on what dates and with which matching conditions? Follow the [SFF profile](entities/sff.md).
- What financial or operational transfers accompanied personnel moving between Conjecture and ControlAI? Follow [ControlAI](entities/controlai.md) and the [policy topic](topics/policy.md).
- Which dated bill drafts, consultation records and parliamentary materials specify each contributor's work? Review the [US proposal](entities/us-ban-asi-proposal.md) and [UK bill](entities/uk-asi-bill.md).
- How do evaluator funding, model-access agreements, publication rights and government contracts operate in practice? Start with [METR](entities/metr.md).
- What original posts and UTC timestamps document the order of amplification around Coxon's resignation? Which financial records address the associated grant and scholarship leads? Start with the [incident topic](topics/incident.md).
- Do Encode AI and Encode Justice represent a rename, different legal entities or another relationship at the relevant dates? Preserve the source names until records establish the mapping.

The canonical dataset also retains the original lead records and their source IDs. Read their wording and source context before promoting any lead or revising its status. Record useful answers in entity profiles and topic pages, and append to the log.
