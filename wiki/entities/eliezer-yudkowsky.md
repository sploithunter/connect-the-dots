# Eliezer Yudkowsky

**MIRI cofounder; bipartisan AI-risk**

MIRI’s about page names him a founder of the organization (then the Singularity Institute) in 2000; the team page lists him as co-founder and a board member. On 13 September 2026 he posted that nothing was more important than making “don’t die to AI” a bipartisan project rather than a Democrat-polarized issue, asking Democrats not to throw barbs and Republicans to treat it as defending home and family. Those posts do not name Coxon or a specific bill.
