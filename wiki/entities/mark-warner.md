# Mark Warner

**July AI framework; companies sounding alarm**

A US senator from Virginia and vice chair of Senate Intelligence. On 14 September 2026 he said even the companies building AI are sounding the alarm and that Congress cannot sit on the sidelines, linking his 21 July 2026 “Framework for America’s AI Future.” That July package predates Coxon and covers data-center infrastructure, competition and safety, workforce, and national security, including a Secure AI Development Act for mandatory secure testing of advanced models. Lead-with-guardrails, not a ban.
