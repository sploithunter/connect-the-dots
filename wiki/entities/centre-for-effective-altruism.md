# Centre for Effective Altruism

**EA community organisation**

An organisation that promotes effective altruism and supports the community through conferences, groups, the EA Forum and, via EA Funds, grants. It was created as an umbrella name in 2012 and later became a project of Effective Ventures after the umbrella charity was renamed. MacAskill’s site names him a cofounder.
