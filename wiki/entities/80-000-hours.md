# 80,000 Hours

**EA career-advice nonprofit**

A nonprofit that advises people on high-impact careers, with a current focus on AI. It says it is part of the effective altruism community, that Coefficient Giving is its biggest funder, and that it was an Effective Ventures project until 1 April 2025. MacAskill is named as a founder.
