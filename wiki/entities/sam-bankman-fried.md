# Sam Bankman-Fried

**FTX; public EA proponent**

The lead investor named in Anthropic’s Series B and an investor named in Conjecture’s launch. BBC and related reporting describe him as a public effective-altruism proponent who said he aimed to earn in order to give. That public identification is recorded separately from the company investments and from later criminal proceedings.
