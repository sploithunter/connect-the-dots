# Bernie Sanders

**US ASI ban/pause co-sponsor**

A US senator who jointly announced the Ban Artificial Superintelligence proposal with Greg Casar on 3 September 2026, before Coxon’s resignation. On 9 September he quoted Coxon, said the builders themselves admit the technology could threaten humanity, and said he would introduce legislation to ban superintelligence and pause AI development — the already-previewed proposal, not a new bill created by the thread.
