# Effective Altruism

**Movement and self-label, not a roll**

A philosophy and community that CEA describes as using evidence and reason to help others. This node is a movement label, not a membership list or a single legal entity. Founders and organisations that publicly identify with it are recorded on separate edges. Taking money from an EA-aligned funder is not treated as proof that a person is an EA.

Profile sources: [W1](../sources/W1.md), [W5](../sources/W5.md).
