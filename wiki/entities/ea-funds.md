# EA Funds

**CEA grantmaking platform**

A grantmaking platform that CEA describes as providing grants to projects aligned with effective altruist principles. The LTFF page also identifies EA Funds as an Effective Ventures project. It housed expert-managed funds including the Long-Term Future Fund. This node is the platform, not every grantee.

Profile sources: [W5](../sources/W5.md), [W6](../sources/W6.md).
