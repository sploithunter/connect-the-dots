# GiveWell

**Charity evaluator; Karnofsky co-founded**

A nonprofit that researches cost-effective giving opportunities. Karnofsky’s Carnegie biography names him as a cofounder and former co-executive director. GiveWell predates the later ‘effective altruism’ brand; it is recorded as a founding institution in this network, not as proof that every GiveWell donor is an EA.
