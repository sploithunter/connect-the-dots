# MIRI

**AI safety research; Yudkowsky cofounded**

The Machine Intelligence Research Institute, a Berkeley nonprofit that describes its work as preventing extinction from artificial superintelligence. Its about page names Eliezer Yudkowsky as a founder in 2000; the team page lists him as co-founder and a board member. MIRI also appears in SFF’s 2025 recommendations with Tallinn as a participating funder. Those are founder, governance and funding-recommendation records.
