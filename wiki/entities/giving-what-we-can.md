# Giving What We Can

**10% giving pledge organisation**

A giving organisation whose members pledge a share of income to charities it regards as effective. Its about page names Toby Ord and Will MacAskill as founders in 2009 and describes GWWC as one of the first organisations to embody effective altruism. 80,000 Hours still lists GWWC among Effective Ventures umbrella projects.

Profile sources: [W13](../sources/W13.md), [W2](../sources/W2.md), [W1](../sources/W1.md), [W3](../sources/W3.md).
