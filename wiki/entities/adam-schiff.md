# Adam Schiff

**In-company monitors; pace, don’t lock in**

A US senator from California whose 13 September 2026 thread said that week’s news and warnings made it fair to ask whether Congress is asleep heading toward AGI. He said experts and executives were calling out frontier-AI risks, that he had spent four years hearing from California companies and researchers about an existential threat, that rogue collusive agents were a harbinger, and that self-regulation had already failed on social media. He wants legislation requiring independent monitors inside AI companies and pacing of new developments, without locking in monopolies or ceding ground to China, plus work on employment, tax and energy. That is regulate-and-compete, not a ban. The thread does not name Coxon. In-company monitors are not treated here as the same thing as METR.
