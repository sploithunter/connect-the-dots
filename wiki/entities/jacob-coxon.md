# Jacob Coxon

**Former Anthropic pretraining researcher**

Jacob Coxon is a former Anthropic pretraining researcher, not a safety-team lead, whose 8 September 2026 resignation is the public entry point for this investigation rather than a core funding node. He spent about four months at Anthropic after earlier OpenAI pretraining work, including a credit on OpenAI’s GPT-4o contributor list, and told Axios he left before Anthropic’s six-month equity vest while still holding OpenAI equity. Writing as @hilbertspaess, he posted that neither company was acting responsibly; a Wall Street Journal exclusive quoting him appeared before that thread, with recaps disagreeing on whether the gap was about eight or eighteen minutes. Later commentary naming first-wave amplifiers is recorded separately and is not part of this profile’s factual claim.
