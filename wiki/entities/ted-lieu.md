# Ted Lieu

**Kill-switch; subpoena AI leaders**

A US representative whose 9 September Coxon-window post, as quoted by HuffPost, told Republican leadership to take up the bipartisan AI Kill Switch Bill. On 14 September he quote-posted Bloomberg’s report that Speaker Johnson wanted one big meeting before AI legislation, and said the majority could issue subpoenas to AI industry leaders immediately instead of adding House vacation. The US kill-switch push remains separate from the UK amendment already in the graph.
