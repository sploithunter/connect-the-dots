# Will MacAskill

**EA cofounder; EV / FTX Fund roles**

A moral philosopher who writes that he helped start effective altruism and cofounded Giving What We Can, 80,000 Hours and the Centre for Effective Altruism. He was an Effective Ventures trustee and a named FTX Future Fund team member who resigned in November 2022. Those are founder, trustee and employment records, not a claim that he directed later lab resignations.
