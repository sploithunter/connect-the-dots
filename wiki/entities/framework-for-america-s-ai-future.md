# Framework for America's AI Future

**Warner July 2026 AI agenda**

A legislative package Warner announced on 21 July 2026 to set rules of the road for AI while keeping US leadership. The press release groups bills on data-center disclosure, AI agents, CSAM/NCII, workforce transition, STEM doctorates, and a Secure AI Development Act for mandatory secure testing of frontier models plus incident reporting. His 14 September post points back to this agenda; it is not a Coxon-week introduction.
