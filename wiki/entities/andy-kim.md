# Andy Kim

**Senate AI guardrails, Coxon window**

A US senator who, in the days after the Coxon warnings, called for Senate-wide discussion and bipartisan AI legislation, saying the technology is moving faster than its creators can control. He pointed to his MATCH and BLADE bills, child-protection proposals, and a protocol to Mythos-proof critical infrastructure. That is a regulate-and-compete lane, not the Sanders–Casar ban/pause.
