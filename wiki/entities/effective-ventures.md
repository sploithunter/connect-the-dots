# Effective Ventures

**EA umbrella charity (UK/US)**

The legal umbrella, formerly named Centre for Effective Altruism, that housed CEA, 80,000 Hours, Giving What We Can and other projects. Zach Robinson led EV US when appointed to Anthropic’s trust. An EV retrospective says trustees MacAskill and Beckstead also held FTX Foundation roles and that Longview and GovAI were among projects in the group. Taking an EV job is not the same as a public ‘I am an EA’ statement.

Profile sources: [A4](../sources/A4.md), [W4](../sources/W4.md), [W10](../sources/W10.md), [W3](../sources/W3.md).
