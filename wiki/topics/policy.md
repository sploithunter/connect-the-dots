# Policy development and legislative work

[Wiki home](../index.md) · [Funding](funding.md) · [Incident](incident.md)

[ControlAI](../entities/controlai.md) describes drafting the [UK ASI Bill](../entities/uk-asi-bill.md), consulting on the [US Ban ASI proposal](../entities/us-ban-asi-proposal.md), and drafting a [UK AI kill-switch amendment](../entities/uk-ai-kill-switch-amendment.md). These are organization-attributed descriptions of different contributions. The UK bill also has an official parliamentary record linked to sponsor Alex Sobel. [C3](../sources/C3.md), [C5](../sources/C5.md), [L3](../sources/L3.md).

The US proposal was jointly announced by [Bernie Sanders](../entities/bernie-sanders.md) and [Greg Casar](../entities/greg-casar.md) on 3 September, before Coxon’s resignation. After the thread they quoted the warnings and demanded hearings and passage of that already-previewed ban. Other members posting in the same window are not a single bloc: some want a pause/ban on superintelligence, others want regulation, kill switches, or special-session oversight while the US continues to develop systems. Later in the week, [Adam Schiff](../entities/adam-schiff.md) argued for independent in-company monitors and mandatory pacing, [Mark Warner](../entities/mark-warner.md) pointed to his July [Framework for America's AI Future](../entities/framework-for-america-s-ai-future.md), and [Ted Lieu](../entities/ted-lieu.md) told [Mike Johnson](../entities/mike-johnson.md) to use subpoena power instead of delaying for one meeting. Follow the [incident topic](incident.md) for that snapshot and the Coxon-window Congress view.
