The investment records connect [Sam Bankman-Fried](../entities/sam-bankman-fried.md) to both [Anthropic](../entities/anthropic.md), where he led the Series B, and [Conjecture](../entities/conjecture.md), where the founding announcement names him as an investor. The source records describe separate company investments. [A2](../sources/A2.md), [C1](../sources/C1.md).

[Jaan Tallinn](../entities/jaan-tallinn.md) appears across early Anthropic financing, participating-funder records for [SFF](../entities/sff.md), and reporting on financial backing for [ControlAI](../entities/controlai.md). SFF's recommendations provide paths to research, evaluation, policy and journalism recipients. Keep the recommended amounts and matching conditions attached to the recommendation date; follow-up payment records can extend that branch. [A1](../sources/A1.md), [A2](../sources/A2.md), [S1](../sources/S1.md), [S2](../sources/S2.md), [C4](../sources/C4.md).

[Dustin Moskovitz](../entities/dustin-moskovitz.md) connects Anthropic investment with [Good Ventures](../entities/good-ventures.md), which he co-founded with [Cari Tuna](../entities/cari-tuna.md). Good Ventures' partnership leads to [Coefficient Giving / Open Philanthropy](../entities/coefficient-giving-open-philanthropy.md) and its disclosed research and policy support. Those routes combine investment and philanthropic relationships; each segment retains its own record. [A1](../sources/A1.md), [G4](../sources/G4.md), [G1](../sources/G1.md).
