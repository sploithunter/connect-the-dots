# Coxon resignation and public discussion

[Jacob Coxon](../entities/jacob-coxon.md) is the public entry point for this investigation: most contemporaneous coverage starts with his resignation, even though he is not a core funding or governance node. The sections below separate participant posts, the news-before-thread sequence, and named amplifiers. Questions about funding and direction remain in the lead records.

## Participant accounts

[Jacob Coxon](../entities/jacob-coxon.md) posted on 8 September 2026 US time (2026-09-09T00:04:00Z) that he had resigned from Anthropic after pretraining work at OpenAI and Anthropic, writing as [@hilbertspaess](../sources/X1.md). Axios reports about four months at Anthropic, departure before the six-month vest, and remaining OpenAI equity. OpenAI’s GPT-4o contributor list names him. [X1](../sources/X1.md), [X9](../sources/X9.md), [O2](../sources/O2.md).

[Evan Hubinger](../entities/evan-hubinger.md) responded publicly with a personal extinction estimate and later clarified the claim about current models. [Joe Benton](../entities/joe-benton.md) said Coxon’s resignation prompted his own departure post and planned move to METR. ControlAI cited the resignation in advocacy. These are dated participant accounts. [X2](../sources/X2.md), [X3](../sources/X3.md), [M5](../sources/M5.md), [C3](../sources/C3.md).

TIME records Coxon saying he hoped to do work in the vein of the [AI Futures Project](../entities/ai-futures-project.md). That is a stated interest, not employment or funding. [X14](../sources/X14.md).

What would close remaining gaps: a primary Anthropic employment-date record independent of interviews, and any original grant or scholarship documents named in the leads.

## Congressional posts in the same window

This is a six-day snapshot of members of Congress who posted after Coxon’s thread, not a census of everyone who has ever mentioned AI. Positions are not identical. [Bernie Sanders](../entities/bernie-sanders.md) and [Greg Casar](../entities/greg-casar.md) used the warnings to demand hearings and passage of the Ban Artificial Superintelligence proposal they had already announced on 3 September. [L10](../sources/L10.md), [L11](../sources/L11.md), [L1](../sources/L1.md).

[Andy Kim](../entities/andy-kim.md), [Chris Murphy](../entities/chris-murphy.md), [Mark Kelly](../entities/mark-kelly.md), [Patty Murray](../entities/patty-murray.md), [Lori Trahan](../entities/lori-trahan.md) and [Ted Lieu](../entities/ted-lieu.md) are in a regulate-now / hearings / guardrails / kill-switch lane that still contemplates continued development. [Pramila Jayapal](../entities/pramila-jayapal.md) called for shutting down dangerous models. [Anna Paulina Luna](../entities/anna-paulina-luna.md) is the most visible Republican in the set calling for an immediate special session while framing a race the US must be ready for. [Chris Van Hollen](../entities/chris-van-hollen.md), [Don Beyer](../entities/don-beyer.md), [Ro Khanna](../entities/ro-khanna.md), [Nathaniel Moran](../entities/nathaniel-moran.md), [Bill Foster](../entities/bill-foster.md), [Delia Ramirez](../entities/delia-ramirez.md) and [Chris Deluzio](../entities/chris-deluzio.md) posted in the same window; some of those posts are shorter wake-up statements. [L7](../sources/L7.md), [L8](../sources/L8.md), [L9](../sources/L9.md).

Later in the same week, not all posts are Coxon quotes. [Ruben Gallego](../entities/ruben-gallego.md) said dangerous AI will not care about nationality and that Congress cannot wait three years. [Adam Schiff](../entities/adam-schiff.md) asked whether Congress is asleep heading toward AGI, and called for legislation requiring independent monitors *inside* AI companies and pacing of new developments without locking in monopolies. [Mark Warner](../entities/mark-warner.md) said the companies building AI are sounding the alarm and pointed to his July [Framework for America's AI Future](../entities/framework-for-america-s-ai-future.md). [Josh Gottheimer](../entities/josh-gottheimer.md) treated Dario Amodei’s slowdown appeal as rich after racing ahead and demanded mandatory national rules. Lieu’s 14 September post quote-posts Bloomberg’s report that [Mike Johnson](../entities/mike-johnson.md) wanted one big meeting before legislation, and says the majority can subpoena AI leaders now. [Eliezer Yudkowsky](../entities/eliezer-yudkowsky.md) is not a member of Congress; he asked that “don’t die to AI” become bipartisan rather than Democrat-polarized. [L17](../sources/L17.md), [L18](../sources/L18.md), [L19](../sources/L19.md), [L20](../sources/L20.md), [L21](../sources/L21.md), [L22](../sources/L22.md), [X17](../sources/X17.md).

Several of these members already had AI bills or talking points before 8 September. The resignation was a high-visibility hook. Critics including Musk called the quiet-account virality a setup to force regulation; that debate is recorded as a lead, not as a finding that the congressional posts were directed. Marsha Blackburn is reported as billed for a related event; no matching high-volume thread from her was captured here.

The graph adds a **Coxon-window Congress** view for this cluster. What would tighten it: original status IDs for HuffPost-quoted posts that still lack URLs, primary text for any Luna–Khanna joint framework, and Johnson’s own status URL independent of the Bloomberg quote.
