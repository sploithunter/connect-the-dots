# Effective altruism: founders, organisations, and money

[Wiki home](../index.md) · [Funding](funding.md) · [Open questions](../open-questions.md)

This graph treats **effective altruism** as a movement label, not a membership roll and not a single legal entity. [CEA](../entities/centre-for-effective-altruism.md) describes it as a philosophy and community that uses evidence and reason to help others. Taking money from an EA-aligned funder is not treated as proof that a person is an EA. Open the **Effective altruism** view for this cluster.

## Self-professed founders and public identification

[Will MacAskill](../entities/will-macaskill.md) writes that he helped start the movement and cofounded [Giving What We Can](../entities/giving-what-we-can.md) (2009), [80,000 Hours](../entities/80-000-hours.md) (2011) and the [Centre for Effective Altruism](../entities/centre-for-effective-altruism.md) (2012). [Toby Ord](../entities/toby-ord.md) is named with him as a GWWC founder; BBC describes their 2009 work as the origin of the movement. [W1](../sources/W1.md), [W13](../sources/W13.md), [W7](../sources/W7.md).

[Sam Bankman-Fried](../entities/sam-bankman-fried.md) publicly identified as an effective altruist and earn-to-give proponent before FTX collapsed. That public identification is recorded separately from Anthropic and Conjecture investments and from later criminal proceedings. [W7](../sources/W7.md).

No self-identification-as-EA edge is added in this ingest for [Holden Karnofsky](../entities/holden-karnofsky.md), [Dustin Moskovitz](../entities/dustin-moskovitz.md), [Ajeya Cotra](../entities/ajeya-cotra.md), Dario or Daniela Amodei, Daniel Kokotajlo, or Evan Hubinger. Their documented links are employment, governance, family or funding.

## Organisations that identify with the community

CEA is the community organisation: conferences, groups, the EA Forum and, it says, grants through [EA Funds](../entities/ea-funds.md). GWWC describes itself as one of the first organisations to embody effective altruism. 80,000 Hours says it is part of the EA community. Those are organisational self-descriptions. [W5](../sources/W5.md), [W13](../sources/W13.md), [W3](../sources/W3.md).

[Effective Ventures](../entities/effective-ventures.md) is the legal umbrella formerly named Centre for Effective Altruism. Present-day CEA is a project, not the same legal entity as that original umbrella name. 80,000 Hours was an EV project until 1 April 2025. An EV retrospective treats [Longview Philanthropy](../entities/longview-philanthropy.md) and [GovAI](../entities/govai.md) as having been in the same legal entity. [Zach Robinson](../entities/zach-robinson.md) led EV US when appointed to Anthropic’s trust. [W4](../sources/W4.md), [W10](../sources/W10.md), [W3](../sources/W3.md), [A4](../sources/A4.md).

MacAskill and [Nick Beckstead](../entities/nick-beckstead.md) are recorded as EV trustees from the 2022 disambiguation and the EV retrospective, which also names their FTX Foundation roles. [FTX Future Fund](../entities/ftx-future-fund.md) personnel in this graph come from the November 2022 resignation statement; reporting treats the fund as Bankman-Fried’s EA-branded giving vehicle. [W4](../sources/W4.md), [W10](../sources/W10.md), [F1](../sources/F1.md).

[Peter Wildeford](../entities/peter-wildeford.md) cofounded [Rethink Priorities](../entities/rethink-priorities.md) and [IAPS](../entities/iaps.md). RP’s CEO announcement names him Chief Advisory Executive at RP-sponsored IAPS; IAPS later said he moved to chief strategy officer. The OfficeChai recap still names him as [AI Policy Network](../entities/ai-policy-network.md). Those names stay unmerged. [W14](../sources/W14.md), [W15](../sources/W15.md), [X10](../sources/X10.md).

## Money: funders and grant vehicles

[Coefficient Giving / Open Philanthropy](../entities/coefficient-giving-open-philanthropy.md) is the principal funder of 80,000 Hours and a major funder of other EA-aligned AI and community work. [Good Ventures](../entities/good-ventures.md), cofounded by Moskovitz and [Cari Tuna](../entities/cari-tuna.md), is Open Philanthropy’s principal funding partner. BBC describes Moskovitz as backing effective-altruist causes; that is a funding path, not a sourced “I am an EA” statement here. [W3](../sources/W3.md), [G4](../sources/G4.md), [W7](../sources/W7.md).

Karnofsky cofounded [GiveWell](../entities/givewell.md) (2007) and Open Philanthropy. GiveWell predates the later “effective altruism” brand; it is a founding institution in this network, not a membership card for every GiveWell donor. [W8](../sources/W8.md).

The [Long-Term Future Fund](../entities/long-term-future-fund.md) was an expert-managed fund on the EA Funds platform. EA Funds later closed it. Claims that [Jacob Coxon](../entities/jacob-coxon.md) received a $20,159 LTFF scholarship remain an **unverified lead**, now attached to the LTFF node rather than to CEA or Open Phil by default. [W6](../sources/W6.md), [X10](../sources/X10.md).

[SFF](../entities/sff.md) recommended IAPS in 2025; a Wildeford IAPS application also names SFF among existing funders. That is grant adjacency. [S2](../sources/S2.md), [W11](../sources/W11.md).

## What this ingest does not treat as “is an EA”

A grant from Open Philanthropy, SFF, LTFF, EV or FTX Future Fund is a funding record. Employment at those organisations is an employment or governance record. Neither is upgraded here into a self-identification. Do not silently merge IAPS with AI Policy Network. Do not treat Anthropic, METR or ControlAI as EA organisations because some staff, trustees or investors also appear in this cluster.
